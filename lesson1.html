Pawn Promotion

When a pawn reaches the opposite end of the board, it can be promoted to a queen, rook, bishop, or knight.

Pawn Structure

Pawns create the backbone of a chess position. Strong pawn structure often leads to a strong game.

En Passant

Pawns can capture en passant, a special move occurring when an opposing pawn moves two squares forward from its starting position.
