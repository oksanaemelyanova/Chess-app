Did you know that

E2 - E4

The King’s Pawn opening is classic and aggressive. It controls the center and opens lines for your bishop and queen.

D2 - D4

The Queen’s Pawn opening is solid and strategic. It lets you develop your pieces safely while controlling the center squares.

C2 - C4

The English Opening is flexible and clever. It allows a slow buildup, controlling the center from the wings and keeping options open.
